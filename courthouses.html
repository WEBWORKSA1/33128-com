Wilkie D. Ferguson, Jr. U.S. Courthouse

400 N. Miami Avenue, Miami, FL 33128
  • U.S. District Court, Southern District of Florida (headquarters)
  • Federal civil and criminal cases
  • Federal jury service for the Miami division
  • U.S. Probation Office

Lawson E. Thomas Courthouse Center
