About 33128.com

Why a ZIP code became a public guide — and what "33128" means.

Our mission

Every weekday, thousands of people walk into the courthouses of ZIP 33128 — jurors, parents in custody disputes, families settling an estate, tenants facing eviction, small-business owners chasing an unpaid invoice. Most have never been to court before. 33128.com exists to make that first visit less confusing: where to go, what to bring, which deadline matters and who can help.

What does 33128 mean?

In geography and commerce, 33128 is the U.S. ZIP code for Miami's Government Center district — home to the headquarters of the U.S. District Court and U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of Florida, the county's family, probate, juvenile and recording offices, and the Stephen P. Clark Government Center. Very few ZIP codes anywhere concentrate this much judicial activity in under half a square mile.

In the economy, it's a renter-heavy, walkable urban core whose daytime population (lawyers, jurors, litigants, county staff) far exceeds its ~9,000 residents — the kind of high-intent foot traffic that powers law offices, bail bonds, court reporting and quick-service retail.

In technology and AI, the digits read naturally as "33 · 128": 128 is 27, the bit-length of AES-128 encryption and a common context-window size (128K tokens) for modern language models. We like the coincidence: this site is about turning complex information into something compact and usable.

For people, it's a place where life-changing decisions happen — which is why plain language matters here more than almost anywhere.

How we make money

We keep the site free through advertising (Google AdSense), sponsorships clearly labeled as such, partnerships with independent attorneys who receive inquiries submitted through our case-review form with the user's consent, and voluntary donations. Advertisers do not influence our guides.

Editorial standards

  • Guides are written in plain English and link to primary sources (courts, clerks, statutes).
  • We date every update and correct errors promptly — report one here.
  • Sponsored content is always labeled.
