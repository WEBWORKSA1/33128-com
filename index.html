FAQ

Common questions

Is 33128.com an official court website?

No. We are an independent guide. We link to official court and clerk websites so you can verify everything.

Is the case review really free?

Yes. There's no charge to submit your situation. Attorneys who contact you set their own fees, and many offer free initial consultations.

What is ZIP code 33128?

33128 is the ZIP code of Miami's Government Center district in downtown Miami — home to federal and county courthouses, the county's main government center and the Government Center Metrorail station.

Can the clerk's office give me legal advice?

No. Clerks can explain procedures and accept filings but can't advise you on strategy. That's what attorneys and legal-aid programs are for.
