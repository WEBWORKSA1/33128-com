# 33128 — Research & Concept Decision

## 1. What "33128" means

| Lens | Finding | Monetization signal |
|---|---|---|
| **Commerce / geography** | US ZIP code for Miami's **Government Center** district (Downtown Miami, Miami-Dade County). ~0.43 sq mi of land. | Very high: one of the densest concentrations of courts in the US |
| **Courts inside 33128** | Wilkie D. Ferguson Jr. U.S. Courthouse (400 N Miami Ave, SDFL HQ); C. Clyde Atkins U.S. Courthouse (301 N Miami Ave, U.S. Bankruptcy Court HQ); Lawson E. Thomas Courthouse Center (175 NW 1st Ave: family, domestic violence, probate on the 14th floor); Courthouse East (22 NW 1st St: County Recorder); Osvaldo N. Soto Justice Center (20 NW 1st Ave); Children's Courthouse (155 NW 3rd St); Stephen P. Clark Government Center (111 NW 1st St) | Legal keywords are the most expensive in Google Ads |
| **Economy** | ~8,000–9,900 residents; median household income ~$36K; ~97.6% renter-occupied; Walk Score 85. The daytime population of jurors, litigants, attorneys and county staff far exceeds the number of residents | Eviction, small claims and traffic topics are mass-market; injury, family, bankruptcy and probate are high value |
| **Business** | The foot traffic supports law firms, bail bonds, process servers, court reporters, notaries, translators and parking businesses | Local sponsorship and directory revenue |
| **AI / tech** | Reads as "33 · 128": 128 = 2⁷, AES-128, 128K-token context windows | Brand story only; low commercial intent |
| **People** | Courts handle life events (divorce, death, debt, injury, arrest) for multilingual Miami (English, Spanish, Haitian Creole, Portuguese) | Strong trust and lead-conversion potential |

**Market data driving the decision**
- Miami personal-injury Google Ads CPCs are reported at **$250–$400+**, with some estimates above **$550** per click. The national legal CPC average is about **$9.87** (2026).
- Personal-injury leads sell for about **$50–$125 shared and $200–$350 exclusive**. The average across paid channels is about **$284 per lead** (2026).
- Florida small-claims limit is **$8,000** (Miami-Dade Consumer Protection).

## 2. Winning concept

**33128.com: Miami Courthouse District Guide + Free Case Review**

An independent, plain-English hub for anyone who has business in the 33128 courts: a building finder, guides, deadline tools and videos. Every page funnels to a **multi-step, consent-captured legal lead form**, which is sold or routed to attorneys.

**Revenue stack**
1. AdSense on legal content. This is one of the top-RPM niches because advertisers bid on legal intent.
2. Attorney leads: pay-per-lead, practice-area sponsorships and district partnerships (`for-attorneys.html`).
3. Local sponsors and a paid directory: bail bonds, court reporters, parking (`downtown.html`, `support.html#sponsor`).
4. Donations, the newsletter list, and contests that sponsors fund.
5. Domain/website acquisition inquiries (the top bar links to web.works/contact).

**Why not the alternatives:** a generic Miami real-estate site faces brutal competition (Zillow, Redfin). An AI or number-meaning site has no buyer intent. The courthouse angle is unique to this exact ZIP, which makes it defensible.
