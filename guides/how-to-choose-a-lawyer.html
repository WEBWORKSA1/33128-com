How to Choose a Lawyer in Miami: 12 Questions to Ask at Your First Consultation

Verify a Florida attorney, understand hourly vs. flat vs. contingency fees, and use these 12 consultation questions to hire the right lawyer for your case.

By the 33128.com editorial team · Updated November 2026 · 6 min read

Miami has thousands of practicing attorneys. The right one for your matter combines relevant experience, clear communication and a fee arrangement you understand.

Verify first

Every lawyer practicing Florida law must be a member in good standing of The Florida Bar. Its public member search shows admission date, contact information and any public discipline. Federal practice also requires admission to the relevant federal court.

Understand the fee models

ModelCommon inWhat to ask
Hourly + retainerFamily, business litigationRates for partner/associate/paralegal; billing increments; replenishment
Flat feeSimple wills, uncontested divorce, Chapter 7, trafficExactly what's included and what triggers extra fees
ContingencyPersonal injury, some employment casesPercentage at each stage; who pays costs if you lose

12 questions to ask

  1. How many cases like mine have you handled in the last two years?
  2. Who will actually work on my file day to day?
  3. What's your honest assessment of my strengths and weaknesses?
  4. What outcomes are realistic — best, likely and worst case?
  5. What's the expected timeline?
  6. How do you bill, and can I get the agreement in writing?
  7. What costs (filing, experts, depositions) should I expect beyond your fee?
  8. How and how quickly do you return calls and emails?
  9. Do you see a path to settle or resolve this without trial?
  10. What do you need from me right now?
  11. Are there deadlines I'm at risk of missing?
  12. If we disagree on strategy, how do we handle it?

Red flags

  • Guarantees of a specific result.
  • Pressure to sign on the spot.
  • Vague fee terms or refusal to put them in writing.
