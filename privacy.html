Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026.

This policy explains what 33128.com ("we") collects and how it's used.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (case review, contact, sponsorship, donation pledge, contest entry, application or newsletter), we receive the information you enter. Case-review submissions are shared, with your express consent, with one or more independent attorneys or legal-service providers so they can contact you. We do not sell your information to data brokers.

Form processing

Forms are delivered through a third-party form-processing service that transmits submissions to our private inbox. That service processes data under its own privacy policy.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info. See also how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Choosing "Essential only" in our cookie banner requests non-personalized ads. We store your theme and consent choices in your browser's local storage.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube using privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you click play.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

You may ask us to access or delete information you submitted by using the contact form. California, Virginia, Colorado and other state residents may have additional rights under applicable law.

Security

Our site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission is 100% secure; please don't submit Social Security, bank or card numbers.

Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the date above.
