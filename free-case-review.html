Questions about the review

Does it cost anything?

No. Submitting is free. If you decide to hire an attorney, fees are agreed between you and that attorney — and many injury lawyers charge nothing unless they win.

Who sees my information?

Only our intake team and the attorney(s) or legal-service providers best suited to your issue. See our privacy policy.

Should I include documents?

Not yet. The attorney who contacts you will tell you what to send. Please don't include Social Security, bank or card numbers in the form.
